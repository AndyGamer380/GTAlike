# GTAlike

**JDK SHOULD BE INSTALLED ON YOUR PC**

This is a very rudimentary form of GTA. It's called Voxcity. It basically runs on a low resolution raycasting engine. And the mobs, cars and buildings are all cuboidal. The npcs have Minecraft type skin. The weapons sprites are like doom.

**HISTORY**
I really wanted to play it on my PC. But my laptop specs are very low quality. So, I made this gta copy with the help of google ai studio.

**AIM**
This attempt at making a gta copy is specifically aimed at making the experience of this game affordable for those who do not have enough money to buy the original, or do not have good specs of their PC or laptop, and do not have the money to buy one with better specs. 
GTAlike's requirements are still pretty much narrow and specific, because it's just a PC version. Not an Android version. Not an html game. However, this game should set an example for bringing fun experiences closer to your access. 
I envision this game to get more detailed and fun. 
People may download, edit the code, and play it themselves. 
However, there are very specific rules I would strictly want programmers to follow if they ever try to edit it's code. 
Otherwise, this project would simply lose its aim. 
   *RULES:*
    1. Make the story, the cutscenes, the gameplay, the animation more artistic and engaging. 
    2. Keep the system requirements as low as possible. 
    for example, my own laptop's specs are these:
    RAM: 2GB 
    Graphics card: Intel(R) HD graphics
    CPU: Intel Pentium N3700 1.6ghz
    No ssd. 
    And it runs Windows 10. That means the OS itself takes up about 1 GB of ram.
    So only 1 GB free ram is left.
    And even after that, my laptop hangs even when a game like Minecraft alpha v1.x takes up just 100MB of ram, at lowest render distance and fast graphics. 
    It even hangs when I run a 200/300mb pk3 file for gzdoom. And it hangs when I run chrome. 

    Thus, keeping the game within the specs is extremely essential to the aim of this project. One thing you could do is make an n64 rom Or a gameboy rom file that could run a 3d gta copy. Because those emulators run fine on my laptop specs. 

**UPDATES**

The VoxCity_v1.0 is actually the first version.
Do not mistake it for the VoxCity that is used to make voxel maps from real life data of geographical locations. 

UPDATE1
GTAlike_v1.0 actually uses magicavoxel  .vox files for maps.
It has few issues:
1. Player noclips through one block higher obstacles instead of climbing up.
2. No music or sound effects.
3. cars kinda get lumped in places.
4. There is no particular storyline yet. 

UPDATE2
Player, cars and NPCs do not noclip into blocks. They can properly climb up one block obstacles. and they also fall down from obstacles.
